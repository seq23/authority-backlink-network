# Dream Wedding Builder and City Vendor Architecture

## Locked model

- One brand ID: `dream-wedding-builder`.
- Four owned domains: `weddingchecklistpdf.com`, `weddingbudgetspreadsheet.com`, `weddingtimelinetemplate.com`, and `weddingseatingchartmaker.com`.
- Five routes: `/build`, `/products/checklist-pdf`, `/products/budget-spreadsheet`, `/products/timeline-template`, and `/products/seating-chart-maker`.
- Any owned domain may serve any registered route. Preferred domains are used by default.
- Memphis Vendor Library remains live and continues to support Porch & Party.
- City Vendor Library is the reusable publication family; Memphis is the first active instance.
- Candidate cities cannot publish until approved, built, validated, and marked active.
- Ordinary articles use one primary wedding-product backlink by default and remain useful without it.
