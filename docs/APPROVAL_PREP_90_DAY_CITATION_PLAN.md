# Approval Prep 90-Day Citation Plan

## Product model
Approval Prep is a self-service document-creation business. It sells editable letter and document kits that users complete, review, download, and send themselves. The free Letter Writing Studio supports a first draft.

**Primary line:** Create the letter. Build the packet. Get ready before you apply.

## Growth objective
Publish 5–8 useful Approval Prep authority pages per day from day one, targeting 100,000 discoverable citation surfaces or impressions in 90 days without relabeling them as 100,000 independent backlinks.

## Destination priority
1. Paid document kits: about 55%
2. Free Letter Writing Studio: about 15%
3. High-intent educational guides: about 20%
4. Trust and boundary pages: about 10%

These are strategy targets, not release blockers. Every page must identify a real document problem and link to a matching product or guide.

## Product families
- Letter of Explanation Kit
- Income + Employment Letter Kit
- Credit Letter Kit
- Rental Application Kit
- Loan Prep Letter Kit
- Business Funding Prep Kit
- Life Admin Letter Kit
- Complete Approval Prep Bundle
- Free Letter Writing Studio

## Compliance boundary
Approval Prep does not repair credit, contact third parties, provide legal or financial advice, create fake documents, verify income, or guarantee approval, deletion, or score improvement.

## Validation tolerance

The cadence, page count, word count, anchor length, destination mix, and product distribution are operating targets with normal margins of error. They do not block publication. Hard failures are reserved for unreadable or invalid files, wrong-domain routing, false published-link evidence, missing required product identity, or clearly prohibited claims such as guaranteed approval, fake documents, or third-party representation.
