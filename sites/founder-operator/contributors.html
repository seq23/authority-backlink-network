Contributors and byline policy

Who writes for Founder Operator Library, how bylines are used, and how to write for it.

Short answer

Every page here is bylined Founder Operator Library Editorial Desk, the editorial company for this publication. This publication has no external contributors today. Every page is produced by the publisher and the automated system described in the editorial standards. When a named person writes for this publication they get a byline on their own work and an author page, and nothing else carries their name.

Current contributors

This publication has no external contributors today. Every page is produced by the publisher and the automated system described in the editorial standards. There is no contributor list below because there are no contributors, and inventing one would be the fastest way to make everything else on this page worthless.

How bylines work here

A named contributor gets a byline on their own work, an author page, and a link to a profile they control. Bylines are never applied to pages the named person did not write.

Pages produced by the automated system described in the statement of editorial standards do not carry a personal byline, because no person wrote them. They are attributed to Founder Operator Library Editorial Desk, which is answerable for them as an organisation. That distinction is the whole point of having a byline system at all: a person's name on a page has to mean that person is answerable for it, or it means nothing. The same name appears in this page's machine-readable metadata as the author, so an automated reader and a human reader are told the same thing.

What a contributor needs

  • A real, verifiable identity and at least one external profile the contributor controls.
  • Named, on-the-record sourcing. Anonymous 'industry experts' are not published.
  • A written disclosure of any commercial interest in the subject, published on the page.

A contributor keeps the copyright in their own work and can have it removed if the relationship ends. Their author page links to a profile they control, so the credit follows them rather than being trapped here.

What this publication offers

  • A byline, an author page, and a link to a profile the contributor controls.
  • Editing for accuracy and scope, not for search placement.
  • No obligation to mention, cite or favour any affiliated project. A contributor who wants nothing to do with the affiliated projects can write a page that cites none of them.
  • A published correction if this publication gets something wrong in their work.

How to write for this publication

Write to pitch@founderoperatorlibrary.com with the question you want to answer, why you are the person to answer it, and where your work can be read. Pitches that are really link requests are declined, and a pitch that offers a payment in either direction is declined automatically.
