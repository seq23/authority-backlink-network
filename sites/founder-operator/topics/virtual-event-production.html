Virtual event production and executive broadcast

Virtual event production is the coordination of speakers, video, audio, graphics and show cues for an audience watching remotely or across venues. A production brief turns the event format into crew responsibilities, rehearsals and fallback plans, so a buyer can compare quotes against the same deliverables.

Quick answer

Choose the format and speaker locations before requesting bids; compare crew responsibilities, rehearsal coverage and failure handling rather than audience size alone.

What this guide is best for: Planning, running and pricing virtual, hybrid and broadcast events, from run-of-show documents to what actually drives the cost.

Frequently asked questions

How should I compare production bids?

Put the same show specification beside each bid: live sources, presenter locations, prerecorded segments, captions, rehearsal scope and recording delivery. Ask which crew roles cover each responsibility and what happens when a source fails. A low total is difficult to interpret until those assumptions match.

What belongs in the run of show?

Give every cue an owner, trigger, source and fallback. Include what viewers should see during a delayed speaker connection and who authorizes a change. Separate the audience agenda from the technical cue sheet so the production crew can act without interpreting a promotional schedule during the broadcast.

Which standards should I check?

Ask the production provider which audiovisual and entertainment technology standards apply to the proposed equipment and venue. AVIXA and ESTA publish standards resources; their presence in a reading list is not certification of a vendor. Confirm venue restrictions separately before agreeing to staging, power or rigging work.

Where can I check the underlying requirements?

  • AVIXA: Standards - Overview — Published audiovisual standards, for what a competent AV or event production vendor works to.
  • Entertainment Services and Technology Association: ESTA Technical Standards Program — The ANSI-accredited standards programme for entertainment rigging, power and staging.
