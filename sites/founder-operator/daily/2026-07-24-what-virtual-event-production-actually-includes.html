What Virtual Event Production Actually Includes

Virtual event production includes the operating work required to turn a remote program into a stable audience experience: platform configuration, run-of-show design, speaker preparation, graphics and playback, technical direction, moderation, accessibility support, contingency planning, recording, and post-event delivery. It is broader than simply opening a video meeting.

  • Rehearse high-risk transitions and backup procedures.
  • Confirm captioning, recording, analytics, and post-event deliverables.
  • Document platform permissions and escalation paths.

What questions should I ask?

  • Who owns technical direction during the live program?
  • How are speakers prepared and rehearsed?
  • What happens if a presenter, file, or platform fails?
  • Which accessibility services are included?
  • What recordings and reports are delivered afterward?
