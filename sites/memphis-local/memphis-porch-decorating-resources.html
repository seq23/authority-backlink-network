Prepare the porch before design begins

A useful porch plan starts with the space, season, household routine, and maintenance tolerance. Measure doors, steps, railings, and walkways. Note sun, shade, wind, drainage, and any surfaces that cannot be drilled or permanently altered. The design should preserve a safe path for residents, guests, deliveries, and emergency access.

Share whether the installation is for everyday use, a holiday, a party, or a short event window. Temporary installations may prioritize speed and impact, while seasonal designs need materials that can tolerate Memphis weather and repeated use.

Questions for a decorating service

  • Are materials purchased, rented, or reused?
  • Who owns the décor after installation?
  • Are removal and storage included?
  • How are weather damage and replacements handled?
  • What access and preparation are required?

Confirm the total price and the date the porch will be installed and removed. If the décor supports a wedding shower or party, coordinate it with guest arrival, photography, catering deliveries, and any venue or neighborhood restrictions.

Before installation: Remove fragile personal items, identify irrigation or electrical hazards, and confirm whether the service needs water, power, parking, or access while the household is away.

For recurring seasonal service, ask whether the vendor keeps an inventory and photographs prior installations. A simple record reduces waste, supports reuse, and makes the next design conversation more efficient.
