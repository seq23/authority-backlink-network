Information to gather before requesting a quote

A grazing-table quote depends on more than guest count. Share the event date, service window, venue, indoor or outdoor setting, table size, dietary needs, desired presentation, and whether the vendor supplies serving pieces or décor. Ask how long food may remain displayed and what plan applies in warm weather or during an extended reception.

Clarify whether the service is intended as a full meal, cocktail-hour food, or a visual supplement to other catering. That distinction affects quantity and cost. Also confirm who provides plates, napkins, utensils, labels, refrigeration, replenishment, and cleanup.

Questions that protect the guest experience

  • How are allergens and dietary requests identified?
  • What foods require temperature control?
  • Is setup included, and how much venue access is needed?
  • Will the vendor remove leftovers and serving materials?
  • What happens if the guest count changes?

For weddings and larger parties, place the grazing-table setup on the event timeline and share the venue contact. A beautiful display can still create operational problems when access, power, refrigeration, or cleanup has not been assigned.

Final confirmation: Reconfirm guest count, dietary notes, arrival time, display location, and pickup responsibilities several days before the event so the venue and food provider are working from the same plan.
