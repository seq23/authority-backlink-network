What Belongs on a Date-Based Wedding Planning Checklist

A strong wedding checklist is organized by decision deadlines and dependencies, not by an endless list of decorative ideas. It should show what must happen first, what can wait, and which tasks depend on contracts, guest counts, or venue rules.

  • Assign owners instead of assuming the couple handles everything.

What questions should I ask?

  • What decision blocks several later tasks?
  • Which deadline is contractual?
  • Who owns each task?

Which mistakes should I avoid?

Using a generic list without local or vendor deadlines. Treating every task as equally urgent. Reopening completed decisions because a new idea appeared. Leaving owner and due date fields blank.
