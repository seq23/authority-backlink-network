Paying for Assisted Living in Memphis When Savings Run Low

Assisted living funding is the money available to cover a resident’s housing and support costs. When savings run low in Memphis, compare insurance coverage, veterans benefits where applicable, family contributions and alternative settings. Ask each provider about eligibility and payment policies before a shortfall; Medicaid coverage depends on the program and setting.

Before savings run out, ask the community for its payment and move-out policies, check any long-term care insurance policy, and contact the local aging agency about benefits and alternative settings. Confirm eligibility with the program; do not assume Medicaid will pay the current assisted living bill.

What this guide is best for: paying for senior care

What should I compare?

  • Work out how many months current savings and income cover at today's monthly cost.
  • Ask the current community in writing whether it accepts Medicaid long-term care coverage, and for which unit types.
  • Check for a long-term care insurance policy and what its elimination period is.
  • Find out whether veterans benefits may apply and what records they require.
  • Agree in advance who in the family contributes what, and write it down.

What questions should I ask?

  • Does this community accept Medicaid long-term care coverage, and is there a waiting list?
  • What happens to a resident whose private funds run out here?
  • Which costs rise each year, and by how much historically?
  • Who can help us with an eligibility screening, and is it free?
  • What paperwork should we start gathering now?
