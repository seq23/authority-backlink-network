Wedding day timelines

A wedding-day timeline is a shared schedule linking ceremony and reception events to the people, spaces and setup tasks they depend on. Build it around confirmed venue access and vendor commitments, then add travel, meals and contingency time. Share one version with named contacts so changes do not leave suppliers following different schedules.

How do you prepare before calling vendors?

Collect the ceremony start, venue access window, travel route, photography plans and reception finish. Ask each vendor which setup and departure times are fixed. Put those constraints on the schedule before arranging optional activities around them.

What commonly goes wrong with a timeline?

A ceremony time does not tell a florist when the room is available. Record access, unloading, setup and collection separately. Travel between venues also needs its own block; do not assume the photographer, couple and guests will all arrive together.

Who handles changes on the day?

Name the person who can approve a schedule change and give vendors that contact. Keep the current version in one shared place. If weather changes the ceremony location, confirm the revised setup route and tell every supplier affected, rather than only editing the couple’s copy.

What should the final handoff contain?

Give each supplier their arrival point, access time, on-site contact and dependency on other work. Confirm who releases the space after the event and who collects hired items. The former preparation and mistakes-to-avoid pages covered this same planning task and now redirect here.
