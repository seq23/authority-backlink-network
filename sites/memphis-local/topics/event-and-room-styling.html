Corporate, milestone and room styling

Event and room styling is the planning and installation of decorative elements within a venue’s access, attachment and removal rules. The brief combines the intended occasion with practical constraints such as load-in windows and permitted materials. Hotel setups also require the hotel’s agreement about outside vendors and room access.

Quick answer

Confirm the venue’s written rules and access window before booking the styling; compare quotes only after design, setup and removal responsibilities match.

Frequently asked questions

What should a hotel confirm before a room setup?

Ask whether outside vendors are permitted, how room access is authorized, which materials are prohibited and when the room will be available. Keep the hotel’s confirmation with the vendor brief. A reservation alone does not establish permission for a third party to enter the room or attach decorations to its surfaces.

How do I brief a corporate or milestone event?

Describe the occasion, audience, venue, circulation, brand assets and desired focal points. Include load-in and removal windows and the contact who can approve changes. Give the provider a usable floor plan; promotional photographs can hide power locations, low ceilings or the space needed for guests to move around the installation.

How should a surprise setup be timed?

Work backwards from the reveal through the setup and vendor arrival windows, with the hotel or venue confirming when access becomes possible. Name a person to receive status updates without alerting the guest. Agree what happens if the room is released late so the surprise does not depend on an unconfirmed access assumption.
