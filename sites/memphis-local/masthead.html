Who is responsible for Memphis Vendor Library

Ownership, accountability and how to reach the desk about anything on this site.

Short answer

Memphis Vendor Library is written, edited and published by Memphis Vendor Library Editorial Desk, the editorial company for this publication. That company is the byline on every page here. It is under common ownership with several of the projects this publication cites, which is disclosed here, in every page footer, and next to every affiliated link, because a reader who finds out later has been misled, and a reader who is told up front can weigh it.

Ownership and commercial relationships

Each of these publications is published by its own editorial company, named after the publication. The editorial companies are under common ownership with each other and with several of the projects these publications cite. That common ownership is disclosed on every masthead, in every page footer and next to every affiliated link rather than obscured.

Accountability sits with the editorial company, which is reachable at the editorial and corrections addresses on every page. No individual member of staff is named on this site, and no page claims a named author who did not write it.

The three publications in this group each have their own editorial company, and those companies are commonly owned. They are therefore not independent of each other and do not claim to be. What they are independent of is any payment for coverage: nothing on this site can be bought, and no listing, mention or placement is for sale at any price.

What this publication will not do

  • It will not accept payment to include, exclude, rank or remove anything.
  • It will not publish a review, rating or award. It does not run any.
  • It will not attribute a quote to a person who did not say it.
  • It will not present an affiliated project as an independent recommendation.
  • It will not exchange links with another site for the purpose of ranking.

How to reach the desk

Editorial questions, corrections, pitches and complaints all reach Memphis Vendor Library Editorial Desk at the addresses above. Corrections are acknowledged within five business days. If you believe a page here is wrong, the corrections page is the fastest route and the one that produces a public record.
