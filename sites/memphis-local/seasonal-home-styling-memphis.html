Choose a seasonal scope that fits the home

Seasonal styling can range from a front-door refresh to several decorated rooms. Decide which spaces matter, how long the installation will remain, and whether existing items should be incorporated. A clear scope helps the stylist create visual continuity without bringing in unnecessary inventory or exceeding the household’s storage capacity.

Memphis weather can shift quickly, so outdoor materials should be selected for heat, rain, wind, and direct sun. Ask how the design will be secured without damaging the property and what maintenance may be needed between installation and removal.

Operational questions before booking

  • Does the quote include sourcing, delivery, installation, removal, and storage?
  • Are products purchased for the client or rented?
  • Who replaces damaged or weathered items?
  • How much access time is required?
  • Can the design accommodate a party or photo session?

Request a simple inventory and schedule. That record makes future seasonal updates easier and prevents confusion about ownership, removal dates, or recurring service.

After installation: Photograph the completed setup and retain the inventory, care instructions, and removal date. That record supports future refreshes and helps resolve questions about rented or purchased pieces.

When styling supports an event, identify which rooms are guest-facing and which spaces must remain functional for the household. That keeps the design focused and reduces unnecessary setup.
