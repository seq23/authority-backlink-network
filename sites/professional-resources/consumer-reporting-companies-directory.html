Every Consumer Reporting Company on the CFPB's 2025 List

The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau publishes a list of consumer reporting companies. Its 2025 edition names 64 companies across 11 categories: the three nationwide credit bureaus plus 61 companies that report on employment, tenancy, bank accounts, insurance, medical history, subprime lending, telecoms, retail returns and gambling. This page is that list, in full, on one page. The CFPB records that all 64 provide a free report on request and that 19 of them will place a security freeze.

What this page recommends

Start from the category that matches the decision that went against you. A denied apartment is a tenant screening file, not a credit file, and the 10 companies listed under Tenant Screening are the ones to request it from.

What this page is, and what it is not

This is a transcription. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau compiled and published this list; this publication typed it into one document and did nothing else to it. Every company name, category, self-description, phone number, mailing address, free-report flag and freeze flag below is carried through from the CFPB's own files without edit. Nothing was researched, estimated, ranked or added here.

It is not a ranking, a recommendation, an endorsement, or a verification of any company on it. The CFPB states that the list incorporates the companies' own self-descriptions and that it has not independently verified them. This publication has not verified them either.

The reason to reproduce a list the CFPB already publishes is that the CFPB does not publish it as a page. It publishes a 44-page PDF, a JavaScript-paginated widget spread over three screens, and a CSV download. None of the three can be linked to at the level of one company, quoted without a reader downloading something, or read at all with scripting off. This page can. Each row below carries its own anchor.

Where every value on this page came from

The CFPB's own currency statement, verbatim: “This list is current as of January 2025.” That sentence is the CFPB's, not this publication's, and it is the single most important thing on this page: a directory is only as current as the day the agency last revised it.

What this compilation leaves out, and why

  • The CSV's final three lines, which carry the CFPB's footnote and a pointer back to the PDF in the Company column with every other field blank. They are notes, not companies.
  • The per-company qualifying prose the PDF prints under 'Free report:' and 'Freeze your report:'. The CSV reduces those to Yes/No and this dataset carries the CSV's Yes/No, so the PDF remains the place to read what the Yes is qualified by.
  • Live links to the 61 companies. Their web addresses are printed in full so they can be copied, but they are not anchors. This publication has no relationship with any company on this list, has not assessed any of them, and will not pass a ranking signal to a data broker on the strength of a government list that explicitly says it has not verified them either.
  • Any figure in dollars. The CFPB's PDF contains a few; none is reproduced here, because a compiled table that half-quotes a number is worse than one that omits it.

Being a compiled reference is not the same as being a cited one. Questions people ask

Is this the official CFPB list?

No. The CFPB publishes the list; this page is a transcription of it. The Bureau's own page, PDF and CSV export are linked above, and they are what governs if this page and the source ever disagree.

How current is it?

The CFPB states: "This list is current as of January 2025." That is the edition transcribed here, retrieved on 2026-08-28. No later edition was published at that date.

Does the CFPB vouch for what these companies say they do?

No, and it says so. The Bureau states the list incorporates information from the companies' own self-descriptions that it has not independently verified. This publication has not verified them either.

Which of these companies will give me a report for free?

The CFPB marks all 64 as providing a free report on request. The PDF qualifies several of them company by company, most often with a condition such as the company holding a file on you at all, so read the PDF entry before assuming a report is available.

Which of them will freeze my file?

19 of 64, by the CFPB's Freeze Report field, including the three nationwide bureaus. The remaining 45 are marked No.

Why are the company websites not clickable?

Because this publication has no relationship with any of them and has not assessed any of them. The addresses are printed in full so they can be copied; they are not endorsed by being linked.
