Use these pages to prepare, not to self-diagnose

The library covers decisions where readers often need better questions before contacting a provider. Topics include regulated local services, personal injury research, dental care, hormone and wellness clinics, neuropsychological evaluation, immigration medical exams, equine legal issues, and workplace wellbeing. Each page should help the reader organize information and understand the limits of general guidance.

Requirements, licensure, fees, and provider availability can change. Confirm current information with the relevant agency, licensing body, insurer, court, or qualified professional. A general resource cannot determine the answer for a specific person or matter.

A practical comparison process

  • Write down the decision and the facts already known.
  • Collect records, dates, estimates, and prior recommendations.
  • Confirm the provider’s role, license, and scope.
  • Ask for written costs, boundaries, and next steps.
  • Seek a second opinion when the stakes or uncertainty justify it.

Some external resources are affiliated. Those links are disclosed and are not independent rankings. Readers should evaluate every provider or product on its own terms.
